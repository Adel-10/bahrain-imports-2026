## Customs-code changes between years
The HS 2022 revision shows up as 722 new 8-digit codes in 2022 carrying 2.7% of that year's value, with many old codes only disappearing in 2023. At the 4-digit level no year's changes affect more than 0.12% of value, so models work at 4-digit level or coarser and 8-digit codes are kept for drill-downs.
